PAIRS APART

Every pair needs its space.

A calm logic puzzle about spacing.

Every piece in Pairs Apart has a twin, and the number on its badge says how far apart the two belong. Count the empty spaces along a winding path, work out where each pair fits, and fill the path. No timers and no rush, just the satisfying moment when the last pair falls into place.

How to Play

Free to download, with free levels to play. For iPhone and iPad.

How to play

Three simple rules. The thinking is in how they fit together.

1

The badge is the gap

A piece with a 3 badge has exactly three empty spaces between it and its twin. A 0 badge means the twins sit side by side.

2

Place every pair on the path

Drag pieces onto the board and count along the path, around every turn. Rocks and roped-off spaces can’t hold a piece, but they still count toward the gap.

3

Padlocks are clues

Locked pieces are already in the right place. Count out from them to work out where everything else must go.

Puzzles that grow with you
