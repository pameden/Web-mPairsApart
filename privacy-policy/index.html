Privacy Policy for Pairs Apart

Effective Date: November 23, 2026

In short

Pairs Apart keeps your game on your device. There’s no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we don’t collect your data. Purchases are handled by Apple.

Meg Software (“we,” “us,” or “our”) provides the Pairs Apart mobile application (the “App”). This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, stored, and disclosed when you use the App.

By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information Processed By the App

To run the game, the App processes the following on your device:

  • Game progress: stages completed, levels mastered, hints used, and the last level you played
  • Unfinished puzzles you’ve saved, so you can pick up where you left off
  • Daily puzzle results, including moves, hints used, and your streaks
  • Your hint balance and the history of hints added and used
  • Which collections you own, as reported by the App Store, and the identifiers of App Store purchases that added hints, so each purchase is counted once
  • App preferences, such as the collection and pack you last chose and tips you’ve dismissed

How Information Is Used

Information processed by the App is used solely to operate the App’s features, including to:

  • Save and show your progress, puzzles, and daily results
  • Unlock levels, packs, and collections
  • Keep track of your hint balance
  • Remember your preferences

Data Storage

Pairs Apart stores your data only on your device.

We do not transmit, collect, store, or maintain your game progress or other App data on our own servers or in iCloud. Because this information remains on your device, we do not have access to it.

The App does not require or offer account creation, and we do not maintain a remote user database.

If you back up your device with iCloud Backup or to a computer, that backup may include the App’s data. Backups are handled by Apple and are subject to Apple’s terms and privacy practices.

In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases: collections, which unlock additional levels, and hint packs. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or receive your name, payment card details, billing address, or other payment information.

When you make a purchase or restore purchases, the App receives transaction information from Apple, such as which item was bought and a transaction identifier, and uses it on your device to unlock collections and add hints. We do not receive this information.

Sharing Your Results

When you solve a daily puzzle, you can choose to share a picture of your result. The picture shows the date, the pack, and your moves and hints. It is shared only where and with whom you choose, using your device’s share sheet. We do not receive it.

Ratings and Reviews

The App may ask your device to show Apple’s rating prompt, and its menu can open the App Store so you can write a review. Ratings and reviews are handled by Apple, and we do not receive any information about you from them beyond what Apple publishes on the App Store.

Contacting Support

The App’s Contact Support screen can open your email app with a message addressed to us, and can open pages on this website. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address and message, and use it only to respond to you.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share your App data with anyone. The App uses Apple platform features, such as in-app purchases and the share sheet, that are provided by Apple.

We may disclose information if required to comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable governmental request, or to protect the rights, property, and safety of users, us, or others.

Analytics, Tracking, and Advertising

Pairs Apart does not show ads, and it does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking tools. If analytics, diagnostics, crash reporting, or similar services are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when such practices take effect.

Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous information about the App, such as download counts and, if you have chosen to share it with developers, crash reports. This information does not identify you.

Data Retention

Because App data is stored on your device, it stays there until you remove the App from your device. Collections you bought can be restored afterward with Restore Purchases.

Your Choices and Controls

You may:

  • reset a puzzle you’re playing from the App’s menu
  • choose whether to share your daily results
  • turn off in-app purchases in Screen Time in your device’s Settings app
  • delete the App from your device, which removes its data

If you contact us directly, we may retain correspondence you send for support or administrative purposes.

Children’s Privacy

Pairs Apart is a general-audience puzzle game. The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example by email, please contact us so it can be deleted.

Security

We use commercially reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the App to support the security of information processed by the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

International Users

If you use the App outside the United States, you understand that information may be processed on your device and through Apple platform services subject to applicable laws and Apple’s own terms and privacy practices.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will revise the “Effective Date” above. Your continued use of the App after an updated Privacy Policy becomes effective constitutes your acknowledgment of the revised policy.

Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us using the Support page on this website.
