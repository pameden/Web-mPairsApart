Playing

How do I play?

Every piece has a twin. The number on its badge is how many empty spaces sit between the two along the path. Drag pieces from the tray onto the board until every pair is the right distance apart.

  • Count spaces one way along the path, including around turns.
  • Padlocked pieces are already in the right place. Use them as clues.
  • Rocks, roped-off spaces and other blocked spaces can’t hold a piece, but they still count toward the gap.

You can replay the guided lesson any time from the menu (☰) on the home screen: choose Replay Training. In a puzzle, the menu also has How to Play.

How do Trios work?

In the Trios Collection every piece comes in threes. The badge is the number of empty spaces between each piece and the next one, so all three are evenly spaced. The Trio Trail lesson walks you through it.

Why is a pack or level locked?

Packs open in order. Finish the guided lesson to open The Garden, then master a pack’s Beginner, Easy and Medium levels to open the next pack. Within a pack, each level opens when you finish the one before it.

The Garden’s Beginner, Easy and Medium levels are free. The rest of the Starter Collection opens when you buy it. The Trios Collection appears once you’ve finished The Garden’s Beginner, Easy and Medium levels.

How do I undo a move or start a stage over?

Tap the undo button beside the board to take back your last move. To clear the board and start the stage again, open the menu (☰) in the puzzle and choose Reset Puzzle. Pieces placed with a hint stay where they are, and hints you’ve used aren’t given back.

Hints

How do hints work?

Every stage comes with a free hint, and the hardest Hard and Expert stages (12 to 14) come with two. A hint places one piece in its correct spot. Free hints don’t carry over to the next stage.

When a stage’s free hints are used up, you can use hints from your hint balance. You get three hints when you finish the training lesson, five more with each collection you buy, and you can buy hint packs from the hint store.

A hint may move some of your pieces back to the tray to make room for the piece it places.

Can I use bought hints anywhere?

Yes. Hints from your balance work in any pack and level, and in the daily puzzle.

Why does it say “No Hints Left on This Stage”?

Each stage allows hints for up to half of its pairs, counting free and bought hints together, so there’s always part of the puzzle left for you to solve. Hints you haven’t used stay in your balance for the next stage.

Purchases

What can I buy?
  • Starter Collection and Trios Collection: one-time purchases. Each unlocks every level of its five packs, for good, and adds 5 bonus hints.
  • Hint packs: a Handful of Hints (10), a Bag of Hints (25) or a Chest of Hints (60). These are used up as you play.

There are no subscriptions and no ads. Prices are shown in the app in your local currency.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the menu (☰) on the home screen and choose Restore Purchases. You can also tap Restore Purchases on a collection’s unlock screen. Make sure you’re signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

Restoring brings back collections. Hint packs are used up as you play, so they aren’t restored.

I bought a collection but it’s still locked.
  1. Check that you’re connected to the internet.
  2. Open the menu (☰) on the home screen and choose Restore Purchases.
  3. Make sure you’re signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you used to buy.

Remember that packs still open in order after you buy: master a pack’s Beginner, Easy and Medium levels to open the next one. If a collection is still locked after restoring, email us.

My purchase says “Pending.”

The purchase is waiting for approval, for example from a parent through Ask to Buy, or for a payment problem to be fixed in your Apple Account. It’s added to the game automatically as soon as it’s approved.

My hints didn’t arrive after buying.

If Pairs Apart couldn’t save your hints when you bought them (for example, if your device was almost out of storage), it tells you so, and the App Store delivers the purchase again. Close and reopen Pairs Apart, and the hints are added then. Freeing up some storage space can help. If they still don’t appear, email us.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com, signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy.

Daily Puzzle

How does the daily puzzle work?

Today’s Puzzle appears on the home screen once you’ve finished The Garden’s Easy level. There’s a new puzzle every day, and it changes at midnight where you are. Solve it on consecutive days to build a streak, and tap Calendar to see your streak, your best run, and how each past day went.

The Starter and Trios Collections each have their own daily puzzle and streak.

Does sharing my daily result give away the answer?

No. The shared picture shows the date, the pack, and your moves and hints. It never shows the board.

Progress and Devices

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Pairs Apart has no account, and your progress, saved games, daily results and hint balance aren’t sent to us.

I got a new iPhone or iPad. How do I bring my progress?

Pairs Apart doesn’t sync between devices. If you set up your new device from a backup of your old one (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), your progress comes with it. Collections you bought can always be brought back with Restore Purchases, on any device signed in to the same Apple Account.

What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting Pairs Apart deletes its progress and your hint balance from the device. Collections you bought can be restored with Restore Purchases after you reinstall.

The app said my progress couldn’t be saved or was reset.

This can happen if your device is almost out of storage, or, rarely, if the saved progress was damaged. Freeing up storage space usually fixes it. Collections you bought stay unlocked. If hints you bought are missing, email us and we’ll help.
